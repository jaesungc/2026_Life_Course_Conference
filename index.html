Directions from Hyehwa Station (Line #4)

  1. Leave Hyehwa Station (Line #4) through Exit 1.
  2. Near Exit 1, take the Jongno 07 (종로07) village bus.
  3. Get off at the last stop, Sungkyunkwan University Athletic Field (성균관대운동장).
  4. From the bus stop, it is about a 3-minute walk to Dasan Economics Building (다산경제관 2nd floor).
    Once you enter the Business Administration Building, go to the 4th floor. The 4th floor is directly connected to the 2nd floor of the Dasan Economics Building, where Room 32211 is located.
